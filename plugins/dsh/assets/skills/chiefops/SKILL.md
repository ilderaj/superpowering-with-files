# ChiefOps

ChiefOps is governance-only. Use it when a Chief or delegated primary lane is selected; direct tracked work needs no ChiefOps check-in to complete its own verification. The Trio remains the sole durable task authority; the Host owns lifecycle, continuation, permissions, and authenticated execution evidence.

Restore the bound task's three planning files at entry or recovery. Choose a concrete bounded slice and record decisions, evidence, blockers, and acceptance at milestones. Honor existing authorization within its scope; clarify only missing material decisions or authorization.

For dispatch, frozen scope, worker approval, or recovery, read [delegated execution](references/delegated-execution.md). For an explicitly bound PR monitor, load dev's `references/pr-feedback.md`; it owns read-only observation, current-head gates, and terminal lifecycle.

Accept a delegated candidate only after independent review of relevant evidence and Trio writeback. A selected human gate remains binding. Return acceptance or a blocker with the exact resume condition. This companion is not a runner, scheduler, registry, cross-thread goal controller, or fourth task-state surface, and sets no model-specific execution policy.
